## 3. Seven Confirmed Engineering Disciplines

Acharya N Co operates across seven specific, documented engineering disciplines:

### 01. Mechanical Part Design
- **Focus:** Individual mechanical components engineered to exacting functional parameters, stress limits, and load paths.
- **Deliverables:** Fully dimensioned 3D solid models, GD&T definitions, material selections, and machine toolpath clearance verification for 5-axis CNC, casting, and forging.

### 02. 3D CAD Modelling
- **Focus:** Digital representation of physical components utilizing feature-based parametric solids and curvature-continuous surfacing.
- **Deliverables:** Native `.prt` / `.sldprt` files with fully editable feature trees, neutral exchange solids (STEP AP242, Parasolid `.x_t`, IGES), and high-density tessellated models.

### 03. Sheet Metal Design
- **Focus:** Formed sheet metal enclosures, electronic chassis, and bent structural brackets.
- **Deliverables:** Folded assembly models, flattened DXF patterns for CNC fiber laser cutting, bend deduction calculations, K-factor compensation tables, corner relief notches, and PEM self-clinching fastener layouts.

### 04. Product Design
- **Focus:** Holistic form synthesis combining physical ergonomics, internal electronics packaging (PCB/battery), and mechanical housing structures.
- **Deliverables:** Complex split-housing assemblies, internal PCB standoffs, snap-fit interlocks, screw bosses with brass insert knurling, and DFM/DFA compliance documentation.

### 05. Assembly Design
- **Focus:** Multi-body kinematic architecture defining how components articulate, interface, and lock together.
- **Deliverables:** Constraint-driven multi-body assemblies, static & dynamic interference checks, 3D clearance studies, tolerance stack-up analysis, and structured engineering Bills of Materials (BOM).

### 06. 3D Printer Designs (DFAM)
- **Focus:** Geometry engineered specifically for additive manufacturing (FDM, SLA, SLS, and metal DMLS).
- **Deliverables:** Overhang-free geometry (45° self-supporting chamfers), print-in-place articulated kinematic hinges, thread compensation for slicer kerf, anisotropic layer tensile compensation, and high-density STL / 3MF files.

### 07. Design Optimization
- **Focus:** Iterative refinement to maximize stiffness-to-weight ratios, eliminate stress concentrations, and minimize cycle times.
- **Deliverables:** Generative mass reduction, rib pattern stiffening, stress-relief filleting, uniform wall thickness enforcement, and raw material cost reduction reviews.
